# 🆓 Free Hosting Comparison - Quick Guide

Quick comparison to help you choose the best free hosting for Queen Style with Neon database.

## ⚡ Quick Decision Guide

**Choose Railway if:**
- ✅ You're a beginner
- ✅ You want the easiest setup
- ✅ $5/month credit is acceptable
- ✅ You want best documentation

**Choose FreeHost.run if:**
- ✅ You want 100% free (no credit card)
- ✅ You need always-on service
- ✅ You want simple deployment

**Choose Altare if:**
- ✅ You need 4GB RAM
- ✅ You want persistent storage
- ✅ You want always-on (no sleep)

**Choose Render if:**
- ✅ You don't mind 15min sleep
- ✅ You want reliable service
- ✅ 750 hours/month is enough

**Choose Fly.io if:**
- ✅ You're comfortable with CLI
- ✅ You want global edge network
- ✅ You need more control
